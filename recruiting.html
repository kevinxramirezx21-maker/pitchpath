Recruiting

How new talent gets on the list.

We recruit players of any age. Nominations come from families, club coaches, high school staff, and our own open ID days.

Step What happens Who it is for
1. Interest form Basic bio, position, club, and a film link if you have one. Players, parents, coaches
2. First look Film review or live ID session. Written notes go back to the family. All ages
3. Development plan Three-month focus: technical, physical, tactical. Anyone we keep working with
4. Exposure Showcase, college packet, or trial intro when the level is honest. Ready players only

We do not charge families to get seen. Program fees cover training. Recruiting introductions are earned.
