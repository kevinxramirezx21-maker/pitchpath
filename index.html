What we do

Recruit talent. Then actually develop it.

Scouting without coaching is a dead end. Coaching without a pathway is a loop. We run both.

Recruit

Open ID sessions, club film reviews, and coach nominations. We look at kids just starting and athletes already on a roster.

Develop

Age-right training: first touch, decision speed, strength, film habits, and match IQ. Players leave with a plan, not a pep talk.

Place

Club teams, college coaches, and pro trial windows. We introduce players when the level is real, not when a brochure needs filling.
