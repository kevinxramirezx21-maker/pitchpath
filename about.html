About

A recruiting shop that still trains.

2Touch exists because too many players get seen once and then disappear. We stay with the athlete: evaluate, train, and open the next honest opportunity.

The method

We watch live and on film. We grade technical quality, athletic tools, decision-making, and character. Then we write a development plan the player and family can actually follow.

Girls and boys are recruited on the same standard. Age groups are separated for safety and growth, not status.

Honesty first

If a player is not ready for a level, we say so and show the work that gets them there.

Family included

Parents and guardians get the same report the coaches see. No mystery process.
